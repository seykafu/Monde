## Description

Monde has 210 people, down from 4,800 in its best year. The mill is shut, the branch train stopped calling nine
years ago, and the clocktower has read 4:17 for longer than anyone likes to say. The mayor ran a three-line ad
in the town paper, and you answered it. You step off the twice-weekly ferry on the first day of spring with a
cottage key and a job title nobody can define.

Spring is twenty-five days. You can get the fountain running again, fix the bakery's left oven, light the
streetlamps, and follow the brass plaques a long-dead founder left around town as riddles. Every repair shows.
Shutters go up, a district's colours warm from faded to bright, and new neighbours step off the morning ferry. A
crew calling themselves the Tollmen collect a weekly fee from the shops for "protection", and sooner or later
you will have to do something about that. This playtest ends at the Bloom Fair on Day 25 with a straw poll: if
the town voted for a mayor today, who would it pick?

The people are the game. Odile feeds you first and talks second. Margo the librarian knows every brass plaque by
heart. Tib is eleven, collects riddles, and has quietly done the arithmetic on the school. Each resident is an AI
character with a routine, a history, a few grudges and a memory, so what you say on Tuesday can come back on
Thursday. Pick a reply or type your own line. They talk to each other too, and a new edition of *The Lantern*
lands every morning, written from what actually happened in town the day before.

## Features

- **Talk to anyone you can find.** 210 residents on Day 1, each with their own voice. Pick a reply, or type a
  line of up to 140 characters.
- **They remember.** Residents recall what you said, what you gave them and what you did for the town, and how
  they feel about you shows as hearts. They also make friends, fall out and court one another without you.
- **A morning paper written from yesterday.** Each dawn, *The Lantern* prints a front page built from what really
  happened, with letters to the editor from residents who have opinions.
- **Fix the town and watch it change.** Fund and finish projects: the fountain, the bakery ovens, the
  streetlamps, the market hall and more. Four districts warm in colour as they come back, and you can walk
  into twelve rooms, from the bakery to the Gull & Anchor.
- **Riddles that are fixed and fair.** 25 hand-made riddles (19 brass plaques the founder left around town,
  and 6 from Tib) and nine hand-built puzzles, with hints that nudge before they tell. Their answers are never
  generated.
- **Cartoon scuffles.** Turn-based fights with the Tollmen on the town's own streets. Nobody dies, and you can
  try talking first.
- **Plays offline.** Quiet Mode is the whole game with pre-written lines in place of live residents. Turn on
  Offline in Settings, or just play without an invite code.
- **Made to be read comfortably.** Three text sizes, adjustable typewriter speed, colourblind-safe emotes,
  reduced motion, and a Choices only mode with no typing at all. A folk soundtrack changes by district and
  by hour.

## About the AI

Residents' dialogue, daily plans, letters and the morning paper are written live by Anthropic's Claude Haiku
5.5 through Monde's own server, while the story, riddles and puzzles are written ahead of time and stay fixed.
The art was generated with OpenAI's GPT-6 image generation and edited by hand, and the music was made with Suno.
Live text has guardrails, including a Report line button in conversations, and the whole game plays offline;
the full details are in the [AI disclosure]([DISCLOSURE URL]) and the [privacy notice]([PRIVACY URL]).

## About this playtest

Spring in Monde is a **free, invite-only playtest** of Days 1 to 25, roughly 7 to 9 hours.

- **Invite codes.** [INVITE REQUEST METHOD]. Enter your code under Settings on the title screen. With a code,
  residents speak live. Without one, the whole game still works in Quiet Mode.

  Day 7 and at the end of Spring) if you do. What is collected is listed in the privacy notice.

**Mac app**
- A Mac with Apple silicon. macOS [MIN MACOS VERSION].
- About [DOWNLOAD SIZE] to download.

**In your browser**
- A current desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- Keyboard and mouse. Not designed for phones or tablets.

**Both**
- A window of 1280 by 720 or larger. Press F11 for fullscreen.
- An internet connection for live residents. Quiet Mode needs none.
- Aimed at 60 frames per second at 1080p on a mid-range laptop.

## Controls

**Walking around**
- **WASD** or the **arrow keys**: walk
- **E**: talk, open, pick up, use
- **I**: your bag · **L**: the Town Ledger · **J**: your journal
- **F11**: fullscreen

**In a conversation**
- **1 to 6**, or **arrows or Tab, then Enter**: pick a reply
- **T**, or the **Say something…** button: type your own line (up to 140 characters; Enter to send, Esc to cancel)
- **L**: the conversation log
- **⋯ menu**: Report line, text speed
- **Esc**: leave

The mouse works for menus and buttons.
