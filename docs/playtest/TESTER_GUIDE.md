# Monde tester guide

Thanks for playing Monde. It's a cozy seaside town full of neighbours, with a dry fountain and a long list of things to fix. The residents are live AI characters (Claude), so what you say on Tuesday can come back on Thursday.

You'll play the whole first season, Spring, which is in-game Days 1 to 25. I want to know what breaks and what confuses you.

## Get the game

Download: [Kasey will send you the link]

- **Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later):** Monde-0.1.0-mac-arm64.dmg. Apple menu, About This Mac, then look for "Chip: Apple M…".
- **Intel Mac:** Monde-0.1.0-mac-x64.dmg.

Each file is about 300 MB.

Open the .dmg and drag Monde into Applications. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first open is blocked. You only need to do this once.

- **macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:** try to open Monde once. Then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" and confirm.
- **Older macOS:** right-click (or Control-click) Monde in Applications, choose Open, then Open.

You need an internet connection so the residents can talk for themselves. There's no account and no code to type. The game saves on its own, and progress stays on your Mac.

## Controls

- **WASD or arrow keys:** walk
- **E:** talk to someone or use things
- **J:** Journal (people you've met). **T:** your tasks. **M:** town map. **L:** Town Ledger
- **Esc:** pause menu

In a conversation you can pick a reply, type your own ("Say something…", up to 140 characters), give a gift, or Leave.

## What to do

1. **Play from the start through the end of Spring**, at your own pace. Expect several evenings.
2. **Blockers first.** Anything that stops you: a stuck quest, a screen you can't leave, a crash, a lost save. Tell me the in-game day, where you were, and what you did. A screenshot helps (Cmd+Shift+4).
3. **Then confusion.** Moments you didn't know what to do next, or why something happened.
4. **Then the residents.** Who did you like talking to? Did any line feel wrong? Would you talk to them again?
5. **Answer the two in-game surveys**, one after Day 7 and one at the end of Spring. They're short, and you can skip any question. They only appear if play-event sharing is on, so when the game first asks whether to share play events, please choose yes (or turn it on later in Settings). See "Your privacy" below for what that sends.
6. **Send me your notes.** Text or email Kasey. Rough notes are fine.

## Is the AI connected?

The title screen shows "● Live residents" when it is. If the HUD dot says "offline", or a dialogue panel says "offline · written lines", residents are using pre-written lines. The game still works, but please tell me the day and time it happened.

## Reporting a bad line

In a conversation, open the small "⋯" menu and choose **Report line**. That hides the line and sends it to me, along with what you said just before it. Use it for anything odd, out of character, wrong about the town, or uncomfortable. The AI has guardrails, but they lower the chance of a bad line. They don't rule it out.

## Your privacy

Residents remember what you tell them. To make that work, the lines you pick or type and your character's name and answers go to Monde's server and on to Anthropic, which writes the replies. The server keeps a record of your conversations. Only I can read reported lines. Please don't type real personal information (full names, addresses, phone numbers), and don't type anything you wouldn't want me to be able to read.

Sharing play events is optional and off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, the game sends things like which day you reached, which residents you talked to, puzzles you solved and your survey answers. It does not send what you typed, except the optional note at the end of Spring. Please leave names and contact details out of that note.
